You do not run updates. Ask your agent to connect email and meetings. Those apps send signals. Omnier updates the files in the background.

## What counts as a signal

- Outlook: sent email, received email, calendar events
- Gmail: sent email, received email, and calendar events
- Circleback: meeting transcripts
- LinkedIn: on demand, when you ask your agent to run `linkedin_sync`

## What happens

1. A signal arrives.
2. Noise is dropped (personal mail, receipts, and similar).
3. Omnier loads the related company files.
4. It rewrites the markdown that should change (`context.md`, people, company, tasks).
5. It appends a line to `events.jsonl`.
6. It commits the change.

The current picture lives in the markdown. The raw event lives in `events.jsonl`.
