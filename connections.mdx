Open [Connect](https://app.getomnier.com/connections) in the Omnier app.

## Outlook

1. Click **Connect**.
2. Sign in to Outlook.
3. Turn the toggle on.

When the toggle is on, sent and received emails and calendar events update your context files.

## Circleback

1. Click **Connect**.
2. Sign in to Circleback.

New meeting transcripts update your context files.

## LinkedIn

LinkedIn is an Omnier skill. Ask your agent to run `linkedin_sync`.

You need the Claude or Codex Chrome extension, and you must be logged in to LinkedIn in that browser.
