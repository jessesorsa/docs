Ask your agent to connect these apps. `get_started` does this at the end of onboarding. You can also ask later.

Your agent gives you a sign-in URL. Sign in, then come back to the agent.

## Outlook

Ask your agent to connect Outlook, then to turn capture on.

Sent and received emails and calendar events update your context files.

## Gmail

Ask your agent to connect Gmail, then to turn capture on.

Sent and received emails, including calendar invites, update your context files.

## Circleback

Ask your agent to connect Circleback. Capture is on when it is connected.

New meeting transcripts update your context files.

## LinkedIn

Ask your agent to run `linkedin_sync`. LinkedIn is not connected in the background.

You need the Claude or Codex Chrome extension, and you must be logged in to LinkedIn in that browser.
