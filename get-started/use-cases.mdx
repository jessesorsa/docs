description: "Ways to get more out of Omnier."
icon: "lightbulb"
---

## Start from the deals you are in now

Ask your agent to load `get_started` and create files for the companies you are actively working with. Write only the current situation.

## Prep for a meeting

Ask your agent to read `context.md`, the people files, and open tasks for that company.

## Catch up LinkedIn

Ask your agent to run `linkedin_sync`. It reads the last two weeks in the browser and writes matching CRM files.

## Write your playbook

Put ICP, pipeline stages, and outreach steps in `docs/current_playbook.md`. Ingestion and your agent use this file.

## Draft in company files

Put proposals, meeting prep, and contract drafts in `crm/<company>/files/`. Signal pipelines do not change these files.

## See what changed

Use git history on `context.md`, or search `events.jsonl`, to see how a relationship moved.

## Hand a company to a teammate

The files are the source of truth. Update `owner` on `context.md` and on the open tasks.
