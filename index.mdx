Omnier is the company brain for sales. It is a set of markdown files that stay up to date, so you and your agent work from the current picture.

1. Customer records, playbooks, and notes live in your Omnier workspace as plain markdown files.
2. Email, calendar, and meeting signals update those files in the background.
3. You use the files through your own agent (Claude Code, Codex, or similar).
