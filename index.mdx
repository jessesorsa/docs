Omnier is the company brain for sales.

1. Your customer records, playbooks, and automations live in your Omnier workspace, as plain markdown files.
2. Omnier updates the markdown files from email, calendar, and meeting signals, automatically in the background.
3. Upcoming: run automations from your sales context to make your GTM self-driving.

## Omnier MCP tools

| Tool | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **`load_omnier_skill`** | Loads useful Omnier skills for your agent. |
| **`read_file`** | Reads a file from your workspace. |
| **`write_file`** | Creates or updates a file in your workspace. |
| **`bash`** | Freely runs commands in your workspace. |
