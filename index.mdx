Omnier is the company brain for sales. It turns signals from email, calendar, and meetings into always up-to-date context files for your team and AI agents.

1. Customer records, playbooks, and documents live in your Omnier workspace as plain markdown files.
2. Email, calendar, and meeting signals update those files in the background.
3. You use the files through your own agent (Claude Code, Codex, or similar), or the file view at [app.getomnier.com](https://app.getomnier.com)
